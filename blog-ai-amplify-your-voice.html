AI won't replace your voice — it will amplify it

There is a fear that AI will flatten everyone's writing into the same generic voice. For people who use it well, the opposite is happening.

AI is a thinking accelerant, not a replacement for thinking. The founders who benefit most aren't outsourcing their ideas — they are using AI to pressure-test them, find the sharper angle, and get past the blank page.

Your voice comes from your experience: the specific mistakes you made, the things you noticed that others didn't, the way you frame a problem. AI doesn't have that. You do.

Used badly, AI writes content that sounds like everyone else. Used well, it helps you write more of what only you could write.

The goal isn't to let AI write for you. It's to remove the friction between what you know and what you publish.
