FAQ

Yes. We start with a voice profile built from your existing writing and a conversation, then every draft is edited to match your tone — not a generic AI voice.

No. That's the point. You approve direction and drafts; we handle the heavy lifting and can schedule or hand off posts for you to publish.

No. AI helps us move fast, but a human editor shapes every piece. The result is content that is both efficient and genuinely yours.
