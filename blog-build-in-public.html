Why every founder should build in public

People don't trust logos. They trust other people — especially people who show their thinking.

Building in public means sharing what you are working on, what you are learning, and what isn't working. It feels risky because it exposes you. But that exposure is exactly what builds trust.

When you share a lesson from a failed launch, you are not showing weakness — you are showing judgment. When you explain a decision, you give your audience a reason to believe you.

The founders who win at this are not the ones with the most polished content. They are the ones who are consistent, honest and specific.

You don't need to share everything. Start with one honest post a week about something you actually learned. That is enough to begin.
